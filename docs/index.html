Founding stewardship

Strong stewardship without ownership.

The draft framework makes current founder dependence explicit. During F0, the Founding Steward can provide strong bootstrap coordination and a narrow negative Mission Veto, while founder status creates no automatic salary, grant percentage, Treasury claim, Endowment ownership or organization-wide IP ownership.

04 · Machine-readable governance

Machines can verify constraints. They cannot manufacture authority.

The repository projects institutional state into JSON, JSON-LD, OWL and SHACL, then applies deterministic fail-closed validation. The machine layer is auditable infrastructure around governance — not a replacement for competent adoption, law or human judgment.

06 · Contributor rights

License in. Rights retained.

The proposed CLA architecture separates a submitted proposal from an Accepted Contribution. Contributors retain the rights they own; full project grants vest only at the defined acceptance boundary, and the CLA itself grants no patent rights.

  • Limited review/archive rights while a proposal is under review.
  • Perpetual project grant only for Accepted Contributions.
  • Constrained outbound relicensing rather than unlimited institutional discretion.
  • Repository administration does not create ownership.
