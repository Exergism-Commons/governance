01 · How governance works

From proposal to execution.

A governance action moves through explicit stages. Human rules determine what authority is required; machine-readable records make the resulting state reconstructable and testable. Technical permissions never substitute for the required decision.

Bootstrap rule: while policy/governance-status.json remains operative: false, draft decisions, Members, Founding Steward assignments and delegations cannot be represented as operative merely because GitHub or CI accepted them.
01 · PROPOSE

State the change

Define the exact problem, affected artifacts and domains, legal or institutional effects, alternatives and machine-readable projections that may need to change.

02 · CLASSIFY

Choose the required process

Editorial, descriptive, policy, constitutional, mission-locked, legal-instrument or adoption-state. If reasonable reviewers disagree, use the higher class.

03 · ELECTORATE

Freeze who can decide

Resolve Active Members, class-specific voting seasoning and conflicts at the voting-window opening date. GitHub access, funding and contribution history do not create a vote.

04 · DECIDE

Apply the rule

Open the vote or other required process, authenticate ballots, apply recusals, quorum and the exact approval threshold, plus any guardian or independent-review requirements.

05 · RECORD

Bind the outcome to evidence

Create an immutable GovernanceDecision record that identifies the rule, electorate, ballots, evidence, exact payload and outcome. A stored “approved” flag is not trusted on its own.

06 · VALIDATE

Recompute, do not merely trust

SHACL and deterministic validators test structural and temporal invariants, reconstruct eligibility and reject inconsistent or under-specified authority claims.

07 · DELEGATE

Name the implementation authority

If execution requires an actor or role, create an explicit scoped delegation: source authority, resources, allowed actions, prohibitions, effective dates, expiry and revocation mechanism.

08 · IMPLEMENT

Change the owning domain

Funding, ID, repositories or another domain performs the authorized action in its own canonical system. Governance supplies authority; it does not silently rewrite domain facts.

Start here

I want to propose something.

Open a governance change only for organization-level institutional rules. If the proposal changes a project-specific canonical artifact, use that project's own process instead.

  1. Describe the exact problem.

    Say what is broken or missing, not merely what text you want edited.

  2. Name affected authority.

    Mission Lock, Membership, voting, delegation, contributor rights, treasury, persistent infrastructure, privacy or another institutional layer.

  3. Declare the change class.

    Do not bury constitutional, mission-locked, legal or adoption effects inside refactors.

  4. Update human and machine layers together.

    If the concept exists in policy/**, ontology/** or the machine spec, update the projection or explain why it does not change.

  5. Expose objections and compatibility effects.

    Include alternatives, non-retroactivity consequences and any cross-domain implementation required.

04 · Decision classes

Different decisions need different friction.

Thresholds use exact integer ratios. Conflicted voters are excluded from the effective eligible denominator under the proposed rule, and abstentions count toward quorum but not toward the valid for/against approval denominator where the class uses that denominator.

05 · What happens during a vote

The tally is the end of a proof chain.

The draft machine contract is deliberately stricter than “count some comments on a PR”. It must be possible to reconstruct who was eligible, why anyone was recused, who cast each counted ballot and which exact decision payload they voted on.

01 · WINDOW

Open voting

Bind the decision ID, decision class, governing rule and voting-window opening date.

02 · ELIGIBILITY

Freeze electorate

Reconstruct Membership history and class-specific seasoning at the opening date.

03 · CONFLICTS

Resolve recusals

A denominator-changing recusal needs a content-addressed conflict determination for the same decision and person.

04 · BALLOTS

Authenticate votes

Each counted Member ballot binds decision ID, class, opening date, Member person ID and vote value, plus signature and verification evidence.

05 · RECOMPUTE

Calculate result

The validator recomputes quorum and approval. It does not trust a stored result: approved field.

Conflict evidence

Self-compensation and other direct private interests require recusal. Founder status does not override the conflict rule.

Ballot evidence

A bare {person_id, vote} or another participant's tally cannot establish that a Member actually cast the ballot.

Historical state

Eligibility, Founding Steward authority and delegations are evaluated at the relevant decision/effective date so current-state edits cannot rewrite history.

06 · Worked example

Transfer control of exergism.org.

Suppose EC wants to permanently transfer registrar recovery control or persistent-domain authority. Under the proposed rules this is not ordinary web administration: it is a protected institutional action.

Stable decision identityhttps://id.exergism.org/governance/decision/{stable-id}
01

Proposal

Identify the exact domain authority being transferred, recipient, scope, permanence, risks, alternatives and implementation requirements.

02

Classification

Qualified Approval is mandatory for persistent-domain transfer and identifier-authority transfer. If the proposal also weakens a Mission Lock invariant, the higher Mission-Locked process applies.

03

Electorate + conflicts

Freeze eligible seasoned Members at the opening date and resolve any conflict determinations before computing the effective denominator.

04

Vote

Reach quorum ≥2/3 of effective eligible voters and approval ≥2/3 of valid for/against votes, with authenticated ballots for the exact payload.

05

Decision record

Bind the result, electorate, recusals, ballots, rule version, payload digest and supporting evidence to an immutable GovernanceDecision record.

06

Validation

Machine checks recompute the threshold and verify structural, temporal and authority constraints. Passing validation confirms declared machine-checkable consistency, not legal validity by itself.

07

Delegation + execution

If a specific operator must perform the registrar action, the decision creates or authorizes a scoped delegation. The registrar/domain implementation then executes the approved transfer.

07 · Machine-readable governance

Machines verify constraints. They do not manufacture authority.

Human constitutional and adopted policy text remains authoritative. JSON, RDF, OWL, SHACL and deterministic validation are projections and enforcement aids around that human authority model.

09 · Contributor rights

License in. Rights retained.

The proposed CLA architecture separates a submitted proposal from an Accepted Contribution. Contributors retain the rights they own; full project grants vest only at the defined acceptance boundary, and the CLA itself grants no patent rights.

  • Limited review/archive rights while a proposal is under review.
  • Perpetual project grant only for Accepted Contributions.
  • Constrained outbound relicensing rather than unlimited institutional discretion.
  • Repository administration does not create ownership.
