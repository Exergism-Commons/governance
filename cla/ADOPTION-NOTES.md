# Adoption Notes — Open Institutional Decisions

> This file records decisions that must be made before CLA 1.0 can be finalized. It is deliberately non-operative.

## A. Legal steward form

The public repositories identify **Exergism Commons** as the stewarding organization, but the CLA needs the exact natural/legal person capable of contracting.

Before adoption, decide whether the Steward will be, for example, an association, foundation, company or another legally competent form. Record the exact legal name and succession route rather than using the GitHub organization name as a substitute.

## B. Governing-law strategy

The contributor base can be international while the Steward will exist under a particular legal system. Counsel should choose whether the CLA uses the Steward's home law/forum, a narrower forum clause, arbitration, or another model, and test mandatory-law effects.

## C. Governance-content outbound license

The new `governance` repository and organization `.github` repository should eventually state whether their reusable documentation is licensed under CC BY, CC BY-SA, another documentation license, or a more specialized legal-template policy.

Do not let the CLA silently make this decision.

## D. ECL project-purpose family

Qualified review should confirm that the Project Schedule's `ECL project-purpose family` is sufficiently definite to permit future legal-version development without granting an unlimited unrelated relicensing power.

If counsel recommends a cleaner file-level copyright license for ECL repository documentation/specification material, adopt that policy explicitly and simplify the CLA schedule.

## E. ECL-PL development copyright

ECL-PL needs a copyright license for its architecture/specification/tooling material distinct from its future patent-grant instrument. That outbound copyright choice should be made explicitly before stable CLA coverage is activated.

## F. Enforcement authority

The proposed CLA deliberately does not claim that a non-exclusive license automatically gives the Steward standing to enforce every contributor's copyright.

ECL LAR-10 should test whether the desired enforcement model is:

- per-Licensor/rightsholder;
- Steward administration plus contributor cooperation;
- an additional legally reviewed enforcement authorization; or
- another structure compatible with anti-assignment governance.

Do not insert an exclusive-right transfer merely to make litigation easier without an explicit governance decision.
