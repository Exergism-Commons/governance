# Exergism Commons Individual Contributor License Agreement

**EC-ICLA 1.0-DRAFT**

> **NOT OPERATIVE.** This draft becomes available for acceptance only if an immutable adoption record identifies a competent legal Steward, governing law, effective date, exact Project Schedule and accepted signature/identity method. Until then, this document is a proposal for legal review.

Thank you for contributing to projects stewarded by Exergism Commons. This Agreement records the intellectual-property rights needed to receive and maintain Contributions while preserving contributor ownership and the project's anti-capture commitments.

This is a legally binding agreement when operative and accepted. Read it carefully.

## 1. Definitions

**Agreement** means this exact version of the Exergism Commons Individual Contributor License Agreement together with the exact adoption record and Project Schedule that apply to a Contribution.

**Steward** means the legally competent individual or legal entity expressly identified as the receiving party in the operative adoption record for this Agreement. A GitHub organization, repository name, maintainer role or administrator account is not by itself the Steward.

**Covered Project** means a repository or project expressly listed as covered in the exact Project Schedule in force when You Submit the relevant Contribution.

**Project Schedule** means the exact versioned organization record that identifies Covered Projects, material classes and permitted outbound licensing terms. A mutable branch or `latest` pointer is not sufficient to expand rights in an earlier Contribution.

**Contribution** means an original work of authorship, modification, addition, documentation, data expression, schema, code, legal-drafting text or other copyrightable material that You intentionally Submit for inclusion in or incorporation into a Covered Project and in which You own or control rights necessary for the grants in this Agreement.

Contribution does **not** include:

- an idea, fact, uncopyrightable method or mere link standing alone;
- ordinary discussion, criticism, issue comments or evidence submissions not intentionally offered for incorporation;
- material conspicuously marked `Not a Contribution` or equivalent before submission; or
- third-party material that You do not own, except to the extent You expressly identify it and the Steward separately accepts it under a valid license or permission.

**Submit** means sending a Contribution through a pull request, commit, patch, code-review system or other project channel for the purpose of having the material incorporated into a Covered Project, or otherwise expressly identifying material as a Contribution for that purpose.

**Accepted Contribution** means a Contribution that the Steward has merged, incorporated into canonical project material or a release, or otherwise expressly accepted for project use through the Covered Project's documented acceptance process. Public submission, review, CI execution or temporary storage alone is not acceptance.

**Target Material** means the file, corpus, codebase, specification, legal text, documentation set or other project material into which a Contribution is proposed to be incorporated.

**Outbound Terms** means the license or licensing family that the Project Schedule authorizes the Steward to use for the relevant Target Material and Contribution.

**You** means the individual accepting this Agreement.

## 2. Ownership and grant of rights

### 2.1 You retain ownership

You retain all copyright and related rights that You own in Your Contribution. This Agreement is a license, **not an assignment of copyright**. You remain free to use, modify, publish, license or assign Your own Contribution independently, subject to rights You may have separately granted to others.

### 2.2 Rights while a proposal is under review

Before a Contribution becomes an Accepted Contribution, You grant the Steward only the non-exclusive, royalty-free rights reasonably necessary to receive, store, reproduce, display, test, review and discuss the proposed Contribution for project evaluation, and to preserve the public contribution/review record.

Those pre-acceptance rights do **not** authorize the Steward to incorporate the proposal into a release, sublicense it as project material, or use it in future project versions merely because it was Submitted.

If You withdraw an unaccepted proposal, the project-consideration portion of this pre-acceptance permission ends. The Steward may continue to preserve already-public commits, pull-request text, review history, diffs and equivalent archival material to the limited extent reasonably necessary to maintain the historical public record.

### 2.3 Copyright and related-rights license for Accepted Contributions

When a Contribution becomes an Accepted Contribution, and not before, You grant the Steward a **perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive and irrevocable** license under copyright and other licensable rights protecting works of authorship that You own or control in that Accepted Contribution, with the right to sublicense as permitted by this Agreement, to:

- reproduce and store the Accepted Contribution;
- prepare adaptations, translations, arrangements and derivative works;
- combine the Accepted Contribution with other material;
- publicly display, publicly perform, communicate and make it available;
- publish and distribute it in source, object, rendered, data, documentation or other relevant forms;
- use it in current and future versions, releases, archives, validation systems and derived representations of the Covered Project; and
- exercise equivalent rights necessary to maintain, preserve, release and distribute the Covered Project.

This grant includes the technical transformations reasonably necessary for reproducible builds, schema/format migration, semantic projections, indexing, archival, accessibility and validation.

### 2.4 Outbound-license constraint

The Steward may publicly sublicense or distribute an Accepted Contribution only under:

1. the license(s) governing the Target Material when the Contribution was Submitted;
2. a later version of those same license(s) where the applicable license or the Project Schedule in force at submission expressly permitted use of such later versions;
3. an additional license or defined license family expressly listed for that material class in the Project Schedule in force when the Contribution was Submitted; or
4. other terms to which You later give separate, informed written consent.

For legal/specification-development projects whose target material is itself a license or legal instrument, the Project Schedule may define an outbound **project-purpose family** rather than pretend that the legal text licenses itself. Such a family must be narrow enough to identify the project and the permitted use of Contributions in future versions of that project.

A later Project Schedule **must not retroactively add a new outbound licensing option** for an earlier Contribution unless the Agreement and Project Schedule already applicable to that Contribution expressly authorized that category of successor terms.

Nothing in this Section prevents public preservation of historical versions under the terms that actually governed them.

### 2.5 Moral rights and similar non-economic rights

To the extent applicable law permits, You consent to the editing, modification, translation, combination, technical transformation and publication authorized by this Agreement and agree not to assert waivable moral or similar rights solely to prevent those authorized acts.

This Agreement does not purport to waive or transfer a moral right that applicable law makes non-waivable or non-transferable. Attribution remains subject to the applicable Outbound Terms and project policy.

### 2.6 No patent license under this CLA

**This Agreement grants no patent rights.**

Signing or accepting this Agreement does not make You a Patent Licensor under ECL-PL and does not create an ECL-PL PatentGrantBundle or any other patent grant.

Patent rights may arise independently under a license already governing Target Material (for example, a software license with its own contributor patent clause) or under a separate express patent instrument. Any such patent grant is governed only by its own terms.

### 2.7 No trademark or endorsement grant

Except for factual attribution and preservation of project history, this Agreement grants no trademark, trade-name, logo, certification or endorsement right.

### 2.8 Administration and enforcement cooperation

You authorize the Steward to administer the sublicenses and Outbound Terms that the Steward is legally entitled to grant under this Agreement and, to the extent applicable law recognizes such authority, to take reasonable action concerning compliance with those grants.

This clause does not assign copyright, create an exclusive license, or represent that the Steward automatically has statutory standing to sue for infringement of rights it does not own.

If enforcement of an applicable project license reasonably requires participation by You as a rightsholder, You agree to consider reasonable cooperation. Unless separately agreed, the Steward must bear reasonable out-of-pocket costs it asks You to incur. You are not required by this clause to commence litigation, transfer ownership, or accept personal liability.

## 3. Your representations

You represent, at the time You Submit each Contribution, that:

1. You have legal capacity to enter into this Agreement;
2. You own or control the rights necessary to make the grants in this Agreement;
3. if an employer, client, university, funding body or other person may own or control relevant rights, You have obtained the authorization necessary to contribute, or the actual rightsholder has separately provided the required rights;
4. the grant does not knowingly conflict with another agreement or exclusive right You have granted;
5. You will identify material third-party content rather than present it as Your own Contribution;
6. You will not intentionally Submit confidential information or trade secrets that You are not authorized to disclose; and
7. information You provide for CLA identity/authority verification is accurate to the best of Your knowledge.

You do not represent that a Contribution is error-free or fit for a particular purpose merely by contributing it.

## 4. Third-party and mixed contributions

If a proposed submission includes material You did not author or do not own, the third-party portion is not licensed under this Agreement merely because You submitted it.

You must identify the third-party material and its source/license/permission basis in accordance with the project process. The Steward may require removal, replacement, separate upstream submission, or additional evidence before acceptance.

## 5. AI-assisted contributions

Use of generative or assistive AI does not expand the rights You can grant. You remain responsible for satisfying the provenance, disclosure and third-party-material rules applicable to the Covered Project.

Where authorship or rightsholder status is legally uncertain, You grant only the rights, if any, that You actually own or control and must not represent uncertain provenance as settled. Project-specific rules may require disclosure of material AI assistance.

## 6. Public contribution record and private agreement record

You understand that Covered Projects are developed publicly and that commits, pull requests, review history, sign-offs and other contribution metadata may be retained and redistributed as part of the permanent project record.

The Steward may retain the CLA acceptance and identity/authority evidence reasonably necessary to prove this Agreement. Full legal identity, address, signature and other non-public verification data should be stored in the controlled records system identified by the adoption process rather than committed to a public repository merely for convenience.

## 7. Effective time, future contributions and withdrawal

Once this Agreement is operative and accepted by You, it applies to Contributions You Submit to Covered Projects while this Agreement/version covers them.

For a proposal that has not become an Accepted Contribution, only the limited pre-acceptance rights in Section 2.2 apply. You may withdraw such a proposal from project consideration at any time, subject to the limited archival preservation described there.

The full license in Section 2.3 vests only when the Contribution becomes an Accepted Contribution. Once vested, that license is irrevocable except to the extent mandatory law requires otherwise. You may stop making future Contributions at any time.

Withdrawal does not revoke rights already vested in an Accepted Contribution or rights already validly sublicensed under the applicable Outbound Terms.

## 8. Earlier contributions

This Agreement does not automatically apply to material You submitted before its effective acceptance date.

You may separately confirm that specified earlier Contributions are covered by this Agreement using the legacy-confirmation process. Existing licenses, contribution terms or other grants applicable to historical material remain independently relevant.

## 9. Successor Steward

The Steward may transfer its rights and obligations under this Agreement only to a successor that:

1. is legally competent to assume them;
2. is publicly identified as the successor steward of the relevant Covered Project(s);
3. accepts this Agreement's obligations and outbound constraints in writing;
4. preserves historical agreement/adoption records; and
5. does not use the transfer to retroactively expand the Outbound Terms applicable to existing Contributions.

A repository transfer, account compromise, administrator change, organization rename or mere acquisition of technical control is not by itself such a transfer.

## 10. Changes to this Agreement

Each Agreement version is immutable once adopted for Contributions.

A new version may govern future Contributions after proper adoption and acceptance. A material change to grants, outbound constraints, patent treatment, representations, succession, governing law or dispute terms requires fresh acceptance for future Contributions unless the earlier Agreement expressly provides otherwise.

No new version retroactively changes the rights or obligations attached to an earlier Contribution.

## 11. Disclaimer

Except for the express representations in Section 3, the Contribution is provided **AS IS**, without additional warranties or conditions, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability or rights that applicable law does not permit the parties to exclude.

## 12. Governing law and disputes

The governing law and, if adopted, forum for this Agreement are the exact law/forum stated in the immutable adoption record applicable when You accept this version.

**If no competent Steward and governing-law entry are present in that adoption record, this draft cannot become operative.**

Mandatory rules and non-waivable rights of an applicable jurisdiction continue to apply according to law.

## 13. General terms

If a provision is unenforceable, it is limited only to the extent applicable law permits and otherwise severed to the minimum extent necessary, without expanding the rights granted by another provision.

Failure to enforce a provision once is not a general waiver.

This Agreement, its exact adoption record, the exact Project Schedule applicable to the Contribution, and any separately executed written addendum form the agreement concerning the rights granted here. A project license remains independently applicable according to its own terms.

Electronic acceptance, qualified or other legally recognized electronic signature, and counterparts may be used if the operative adoption process identifies the method and applicable law recognizes it.

## 14. Acceptance record

The operative acceptance process should record at minimum:

- CLA version: `EC-ICLA-1.0`;
- Contributor legal name (private unless publication is expressly agreed);
- public name, if different and desired;
- GitHub account(s) / contribution identity used for checks;
- email or other verified contact used for agreement records;
- whether employer/entity authorization is implicated;
- exact Project Schedule version;
- acceptance method and timestamp; and
- exact adoption-record identifier.

Do not place private identity evidence in a public Git repository solely to satisfy this field list.
