# Contributor License Agreement FAQ

> This FAQ explains the **draft** CLA. It is not the legal instrument. If the CLA is adopted, the exact signed agreement and adoption record control.

## Does Exergism Commons take my copyright?

No. The proposed CLA is non-exclusive. You keep the copyright you own and can keep using or licensing your own Contribution.

## Why have a CLA at all?

The organization has mixed projects and ECL specifically needs a defensible inbound-rights/chain-of-title model. The CLA records what the project may do with a Contribution instead of relying on ambiguous assumptions about GitHub submission.

## Can Exergism Commons turn my Exergism contribution proprietary?

Not under this draft simply because the CLA exists. The outbound constraint ties use to the target license/project family recorded when you contribute. For the current Exergism corpus, that means the CC BY-SA boundary remains relevant.

## Can the organization change the Project Schedule later and relicense my old work however it wants?

No. A later Schedule can govern later Contributions but cannot retroactively add a new outbound option for an older Contribution unless the older agreement already expressly permitted that successor category or you separately consent.

## Does the CLA grant my patents?

No. The CLA expressly grants **no patent rights**.

A target license such as Apache-2.0 may contain its own patent grant, and ECL-PL may eventually provide a separate patent instrument. Those are separate legal bases.

## If I contribute to ECL-PL, do I become a Patent Licensor?

No. Repository contribution and patent licensing are deliberately separated.

## Is this CLA the same as the ECL license?

No. ECL is an outbound copyright/software-rights license for software to which a Licensor applies it. The CLA is an inbound agreement describing rights a contributor gives the institutional Steward to maintain project Contributions.

## Is this a DCO?

No. A DCO is primarily a provenance certification associated with a commit sign-off. This draft is an actual copyright license agreement. A future project may use sign-offs as an additional provenance signal, but a `Signed-off-by` line should not silently be treated as acceptance of a different legal agreement.

## Do issue comments require a CLA?

Ordinary bug reports, questions, criticism, contrary evidence and discussion are not Contributions merely because they are posted. Material intentionally offered for incorporation into project artifacts can become a Contribution.

## What if my employer owns my work?

Then your individual signature cannot grant rights the employer owns. You need the necessary employer authorization, and the organization may require the Entity CLA or other evidence from the actual rightsholder.

## What if I use AI to help write code or text?

AI assistance does not create rights you do not have. Material AI assistance should be disclosed where the project policy requires it, and suspicious third-party similarity should be handled as a provenance issue. See `AI-ASSISTED-CONTRIBUTIONS.md`.

## Can I stop contributing later?

Yes. You can stop making future Contributions at any time. Rights already validly granted for incorporated/distributed Contributions remain in place; otherwise a long-lived public project could become legally revocable commit by commit.

## Does the CLA cover my past Contributions automatically?

No. Past Contributions need their historical rights basis or a separate voluntary legacy confirmation. See `LEGACY-CONTRIBUTIONS.md`.

## Who is the other party to the CLA?

That is intentionally unresolved in the bootstrap draft. A GitHub organization name is not enough. The CLA cannot become operative until an adoption record identifies a legally competent Steward.

## Why not just name a maintainer personally for now?

The point of organization-level governance is institutional continuity rather than accidental personal ownership/control. The draft therefore blocks activation until the project deliberately identifies the legal steward and succession model instead of smuggling that decision into a contributor form.

## Is this legally reviewed?

Not yet. The draft is designed for adversarial review and must not be represented as production-ready until the adoption gate records qualified legal review.
