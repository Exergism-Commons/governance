# Exergism Commons Entity Contributor License Agreement

**EC-ECLA 1.0-DRAFT**

> **NOT OPERATIVE.** This draft becomes available for acceptance only through the same adoption gate as EC-ICLA 1.0.

This Agreement is for a company, association, university, foundation or other legal entity that owns or controls rights in Contributions submitted by its personnel or authorized contributors to Covered Projects.

## 1. Parties and definitions

**Entity** or **You** means the legal entity accepting this Agreement through an authorized representative.

**Steward**, **Covered Project**, **Project Schedule**, **Contribution**, **Submit**, **Target Material** and **Outbound Terms** have the meanings used in the simultaneously adopted `EC-ICLA-1.0`, except that a Contribution under this Entity Agreement is limited to rights that You own or control and that are submitted on Your behalf by an **Authorized Contributor**.

**Authorized Contributor** means an individual whom You have identified through the operative records process as authorized to submit Contributions covered by this Agreement.

## 2. Ownership

You retain all copyright and related rights that You own in Your Contributions. This is a non-exclusive license, not an assignment of copyright or ownership of employee inventions.

This Agreement does not claim rights that belong personally to an employee, contractor, client or other third party and that You do not own or control.

## 3. Copyright and related-rights grant

Subject to the outbound constraint below, You grant the Steward a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive and irrevocable license under copyright and other licensable rights protecting works of authorship that You own or control in each covered Contribution, with the right to sublicense as permitted by this Agreement, to reproduce, modify, adapt, translate, combine, publicly display, publicly perform, communicate, make available, publish, distribute, archive, validate and otherwise use the Contribution as reasonably necessary for the Covered Project.

The grant includes equivalent rights necessary for format/schema migration, reproducible builds, semantic projections, indexing, accessibility and archival preservation.

## 4. Outbound-license constraint

The Steward may publicly sublicense or distribute a Contribution only under:

1. the license(s) governing the Target Material when submitted;
2. later versions expressly permitted by those license(s) or the Project Schedule then in force;
3. additional terms/families expressly listed for that material class in the Project Schedule then in force; or
4. other terms to which You later give separate written consent.

A later Project Schedule cannot retroactively expand the outbound options for an earlier Entity Contribution unless the already-applicable agreement expressly authorized that successor category.

## 5. Patent rights

**This Entity Agreement grants no patent rights.**

Neither execution of this Agreement nor listing an employee as an Authorized Contributor makes You, an affiliate, an employee or the Steward a Patent Licensor under ECL-PL.

A patent grant may arise independently under an outbound/target license or a separate express patent instrument. Any such grant is governed only by its own terms.

## 6. Trademarks and endorsement

No trademark, trade name, logo or endorsement right is granted except what is reasonably necessary for factual attribution and preservation of project history.

## 7. Representations

You represent that:

1. the person accepting this Agreement has authority to bind You;
2. You own or control the rights necessary for the grants made here;
3. Your Authorized Contributors are authorized to submit covered material on Your behalf;
4. the grants do not knowingly conflict with exclusive rights or obligations You have granted elsewhere;
5. You will not knowingly submit third-party or confidential material as if You owned it; and
6. information supplied for agreement verification is accurate to the best of Your knowledge.

## 8. Authorized-Contributor administration

You must maintain or approve the list of Authorized Contributors through the operative records process and promptly notify the Steward when an individual's authority materially changes.

Removal from the authorized list stops that individual from creating new Entity Contributions under this Agreement after the recorded change. It does not revoke rights already granted for earlier Contributions.

An individual's personal Contribution is not converted into an Entity Contribution merely because that individual works for You.

Where rights are split between the individual and the Entity, the Steward may require both an individual CLA and this Entity CLA, or other evidence sufficient to cover the actual rights involved.

## 9. Third-party and mixed material

Material not owned or controlled by You is outside this grant unless the actual rightsholder separately licenses it. You must identify such material and its license/permission basis before incorporation.

## 10. Public records and private entity records

Public contribution history may be retained indefinitely as part of project provenance. Non-public corporate authority documents, signatures, addresses and contact records should remain in the controlled agreement-record system rather than the public repository unless publication is necessary and authorized.

## 11. Term, legacy material and termination of authority

This Agreement applies prospectively to covered Contributions submitted after effective acceptance, unless You separately confirm identified legacy Contributions.

The rights granted for a Contribution are irrevocable once granted, except to the extent mandatory law requires otherwise. You may terminate coverage for future Contributions by notice through the operative process.

## 12. Successor Steward and Agreement changes

The same controlled-successor and non-retroactivity rules stated in EC-ICLA 1.0 apply. Transfer of technical repository control alone is not assignment of this Agreement.

A material new agreement version requires new Entity acceptance for future Contributions.

## 13. Disclaimer, law and general terms

Except for the express representations above, Contributions are provided **AS IS** to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

The exact governing law, forum (if any), legal Steward, Project Schedule, effective date and acceptance method must be stated in the operative adoption record. Without those items this draft is not operative.

Severability, non-waiver, electronic acceptance and relationship-to-project-license rules follow the simultaneously adopted EC-ICLA 1.0 unless an operative Entity adoption record expressly states a legally reviewed difference.

## 14. Entity acceptance record

The controlled record should include at minimum:

- agreement version: `EC-ECLA-1.0`;
- exact legal name and jurisdiction/registration identifier where appropriate;
- authorized signatory and authority basis;
- verified contact;
- exact Project Schedule version;
- initial Authorized Contributor list or controlled reference to it;
- acceptance method and timestamp; and
- exact adoption-record identifier.
